# Research sources — "Is your child ready for…" PDF series

All facts below were pulled exclusively from cambridgeenglish.org (official
pages and PDFs). No tutoring blogs, exam-prep companies, or aggregator sites
were used for factual claims. Nothing required an [UNVERIFIED] flag — every
fact below traces to one of these pages.

## A1 Movers

- Exam format, papers, parts, timing, shield scoring:
  https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/young-learners/paper/movers/format/
- Digital format confirmation (same parts/skills, on-screen Listening +
  Reading & Writing, face-to-face Speaking):
  https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/movers-digital/test-format/
- Official combined wordlist (Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers, A2 Flyers):
  https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/739104-starters-movers-flyers-word-list-2025.pdf
