Teaching philosophy

How I approach the classroom

My teaching philosophy centres on creating an inclusive, student-centered environment that supports language development through differentiated instruction, meaningful content, and strong scaffolding. I believe that building confidence and autonomy in language learners is essential for long-term academic success — particularly in settings where language demands increase across subject areas. Every student I work with brings a different starting point, and my role is to meet them there.
